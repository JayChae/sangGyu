Artist's Notes

Basic Geometric Forms and Organic Forms

Basic geometric forms self-evidently reveal the principles by which they are constituted. Existing with excessive simplicity and clarity, they appear alien.
Organic forms have neither angles nor points. They offer no focal point at which the gaze can converge; instead, they continually deflect it. They are like vast vanishing points that endlessly redirect the gaze beyond the visible, toward what can no longer be seen.

Forms that can no longer be divided; forms that endlessly fragment.
Indivisible, lucid forms; forms that resist comprehension.
Hard, masculine materiality embodies the Constructivist tradition of sculptural form, in which a unified conception exists.
Softly curved forms and organic surfaces embody the encounter between the Real within sensory experience and the material world.

There are no abstract concepts that exist solely in thought. All metaphysical ideas are grounded in the material realities of the world, and the metaphysical originates in the physical.
Life and ideas cannot be separated.
