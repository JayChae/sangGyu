Artist's Notes

Oxidation

Rust is the corrosion and destruction of metal, yet here it becomes an event that gives rise to the surface of the work.
Within the binary opposition of growth and destruction, destruction, relegated to a secondary position, is not simply the opposite of growth but also the possibility of new growth.
Destruction not only diversifies growth but also constitutes the internal negativity through which the very concept of growth is defined.
