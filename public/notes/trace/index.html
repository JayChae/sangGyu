Artist's Notes

Trace

A trace is a mark of a presence that has come and gone.

A trace is a vestige of what has passed, yet it does not belong solely to the past. Inscribed within the present form are relations with what is no longer present here.

The traces left behind reveal the relations through which the form came into being.

To reveal traces is to present the form not as something complete, but as something open to the relations of non-presence that constitute it. In this way, the visible form points toward what eludes present perception.
