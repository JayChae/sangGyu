Artist's Notes

Residual Sensation

We translate sensation into language in order to understand the world, analyze its structures, and assign meaning. Yet the incomprehensible persists within structures that appear clear.

The experience of the incomprehensible—the failure of understanding and interpretation—gives rise to a residual sensation. It is the simultaneous experience of opposing poles: life and death, growth and destruction, plenitude and lack. It can never be fully grasped or reduced to a single meaning.

Experiencing the omissions and failures inherent in translating sensation into language leads to an expansion of sensory perception and a transformation of thought. Confronting a limit opens up the possibility of growth and change.
