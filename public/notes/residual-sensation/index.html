Residual Sensation

To understand the world, we translate sensations into language, analyze structures, and assign meaning. Yet within seemingly intelligible structures, something incomprehensible persists.
Residual sensation is the experience of the failure of understanding and interpretation. It is the simultaneous experience of opposing terms, such as life and death, growth and destruction, fullness and lack. It can never be fully grasped or reduced to a single meaning.
Here, the failure of language leads to an expansion of sensory perception. The revelation of negativity is an expansion of perception.
