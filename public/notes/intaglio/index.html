Artist's Notes

Intaglio

Neither an intaglio nor negative space is truly empty. A hollow fills the space it occupies precisely by being hollowed out. What is carved away conceives and constitutes what remains.

The identity of being can be recognized only through the negativity of absence.

Absence powerfully attests to existence.
